How the bridge is trust-minimized

Neither direction trusts an operator. Each side runs the other chain's light client in a zkVM and verifies one Groth16 proof on-chain.

Kaspa verifies Ethereum
  • SP1 Helios proves the ETH sync-committee (BLS12-381) in-circuit
  • A storage proof binds your exact deposit under the finalized state root
  • Kaspa's 0x20 precompile checks it → mints wETH 1:1
Ethereum verifies Kaspa
  • A sparse-anchor light client PoW-verifies k=49 anchors
  • The burn is bound by acceptance, not mere inclusion
  • The burn is permissionless — no governance key
  • SP1's stock verifier checks it → unlocks ETH
